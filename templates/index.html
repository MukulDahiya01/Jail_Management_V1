Methodology & limitations

How this demonstration was built.

Modeling approach

Six case-review classifiers trained using numeric administrative attributes and categorical process attributes. Offence identifiers and Act–Section lookup labels are excluded. A stratified 75/25 holdout evaluates classification. The trained preprocessing pipelines are saved with each estimator.

Five next-month population regressors use a time-ordered split to reduce future-information leakage. K-Means uses aggregated facility features. Association insights use co-occurrence of facility indicators.

Limitations

The synthetic case-priority label is not legal bailability and has no operational validity. No individual patient, prisoner or legal records are used. Random synthetic patterns cannot establish real-world predictive accuracy. A public dashboard should contain only these fictional records.

For full EDA, PCA, t-SNE, Hierarchical Clustering and DBSCAN, consult the preserved original notebook included in the repository.
